Measuring results

Likes are nice, but quote requests pay the bills. Everything is tied back to leads and jobs.

Track every lead

A unique phone number and tracking link for each platform, plus “How did you hear about us?” on forms and calls.

Know the cost

Cost per lead and cost per job won show which channels are worth the effort.

Review monthly

A one-page report: leads by source, top three posts, website and Google Business Profile activity.

Improve

Do more of what brings quote requests, test one change at a time, and put ad budget behind proven posts.

Tracking setup

Start small, then add layers as the company is ready.

Step 1

Ask on every form and call

Add “How did you hear about us?” to the quote form, with options like Facebook, Instagram, Google search, friend or family, and yard sign. Whoever answers the phone asks it too and logs the answer.

Step 2

Add tracking links

Give each platform its own link to the website, tagged so Google Analytics shows which one sent each visitor and quote request.

Step 3

Add tracking phone numbers

Use a different forwarding number on each profile so the number dialed shows where the caller came from.

Ideas I’d explore

Starting points I’d test with your team, tracking the results before expanding.

Never miss a call

Storm seasons keep phones busy, and a missed call can be a missed job. I’d explore after-hours answering or a missed-call text-back so every caller hears back quickly.

Know where calls come from

Simple tools can transcribe calls and tag them by source, so we can see which posts and platforms lead to booked jobs, not just likes. I’d check state call-recording consent rules first.

Build trust before the call

Clear before/after photos and a simple “what to expect” post about the repair process can help homeowners feel confident, without giving insurance advice.

Show the project before it starts

If the team already uses a measuring and design tool like Hover, 3D color and material previews could become social posts and website visuals, such as “Which siding color would you pick?” I’d only use a homeowner’s property with their permission.

Track leads in the company’s CRM

If the team uses a CRM like AccuLynx, I’d make sure every lead is logged with its source and outcome. That shows which posts and platforms bring booked jobs and feeds the monthly report. I’d work within whatever system the team already uses.

Start small and measure

I’d test one idea at a time, track the results, and only add more once it’s working.
