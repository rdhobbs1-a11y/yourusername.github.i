Why I designed it this way

Notes for the hiring team.

  • Quote first. A free-quote button sits in the header, hero, mobile bar, and its own section.
  • Trust right under the hero. Ratings, certifications, and experience answer the first worry homeowners have.
  • Projects as proof. The before/after slider and gallery double as social content.
  • Built for phones. A sticky call and quote bar (call and quote will come up on the bottom on the phone) keeps the next step one tap away.
  • Easy to track. Each form and social link can carry its own tracking so I can show which channels bring leads.
